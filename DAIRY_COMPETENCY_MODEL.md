# Commercial Dairy Practice — governed curriculum

PROPOSED — REQUIRES DOMAIN EXPERT VALIDATION.

Phase 3E adds a dairy teaching organization over existing reusable assessable child definitions. A curriculum domain is not a new child competency or award. Version identifiers, not labels alone, bind learning and assessment evidence. The original 47 groups/69 child definitions are not replaced or expanded into other pathways.

These are scope outlines, not assertions that every topic has a teaching unit, assessment or validated standard. Full topic lists are preserved in version-bound curriculum metadata and domain views. Orientation, welfare and prevention intentionally reuse broader shared/herd competencies. Narrower assessable outcomes require professional review rather than fabricated child awards.

## Starter learning units

1. **Investigating transition and fresh-cow concerns**: individual clinical versus cohort reasoning, group/timeline definition, records, feed availability versus measured intake, condition, calving, environment and uncertainty. Links to the existing Transition & Fresh Cow case at its exact assessment version.
2. **Investigating calf underperformance**: records, neonatal/colostrum process, feeding, water, housing, hygiene, growth and missing information. An unscored investigation activity; no new calf case assessment is claimed.
3. **Interpreting imperfect dairy records**: a deliberately fictional two-group dataset, trends, missing dates/DIM/parity/measurement context, alternatives and limits of causal inference. An unscored data activity; no spreadsheet proficiency award.

Every unit carries Know → Interpret → Investigate → Decide → Communicate → Demonstrate objectives, prerequisite child-version/facet guidance, clinical/herd/commercial/African context, mistakes, safety/escalation prompts, references, supervised evidence requirements and exact lesson/course version provenance.

Reference leads point to the FAO dairy resource collection. No specific publication, edition, numeric standard or professional claim has been validated against that collection. Year, reviewer and review date remain explicitly unknown. These links must not be described as reviewed authoritative support. No Drive materials were reviewed.

## Evidence, assessment and gaps

Learning activities and reading completion are participation records only. They never generate KNOWLEDGE_ASSESSED, CASE_ASSESSED, PRACTICALLY_VERIFIED, readiness or awards. The linked case uses existing structured reasoning, submission locking, assigned assessors, private drafts, governed rubric dimensions, immutable final feedback, moderation/appeals and evidence provenance. Demo case evidence stays demo and UNVERIFIED. A human review does not automatically professionally verify it.

Candidate dashboard evidence retains facet, observation, status, demo flag and attempt provenance. Prerequisites show verified non-demo non-expired evidence when present; otherwise UNVERIFIED, never INCOMPETENT. Guidance is not an activated prerequisite gate. Suggested next reading follows unfinished lessons, not professional priority weights.

Case reasoning remains Phase 3D's eight categories across ten governed stages: observation, interpretation, assumptions, information requests, priorities, actions, monitoring and safety. Learning prompts also ask for supporting/contradicting evidence, alternative hypotheses and escalation. New cases can use existing Phase 3C authoring/versioned rubric primitives; no parallel clinical case subsystem is introduced.

## Practical development

Supervised history taking, animal/group observation, colostrum/process inspection, body condition, locomotion, feed/water evaluation, herd walk, records and communication remain separate evidence tasks. Phase 3A already records candidate, competency, assessor/context, observation, provenance and limitations. This phase records proposed requirements only; it does not turn online learning or written cases into field verification.

All domain definitions, objectives, prerequisites, case/rubric anchors, contextual recommendations, source choices and practical requirements require veterinary expert validation. Protocols, dosages, withdrawal periods, vaccination schedules, nutrient/DCAD/mineral/SCC/reproduction/growth/disease targets, weights, pass marks, certification and readiness rules are absent/inactive.

## Manual demonstration

1. Apply migrations using existing private local configuration; do not replace environment files.
2. Owner opens `/dairy/review`, prepares/inspects the existing case and explicitly releases its demo if not already released.
3. Owner opens `/dairy/learning/manage`, prepares the starter, inspects exact version/metadata and explicitly releases DRAFT for demonstration. This does not approve professional content.
4. Candidate saves a profile/selects dairy via `/dairy`, opens `/dairy/learning`, enrolls in the exact curriculum version, reads objectives/context/source warnings, saves an activity and reading position, then reloads/resumes.
5. From the transition unit, start/resume the linked case. Save every stage, submit, assign an independent assessor using existing tools, save/finalize review and inspect feedback/evidence on `/dairy`.
6. Confirm that learning responses are not assessment awards; case evidence is demo/unverified and practical/readiness/certification remain separate/inactive. Test interrupted saving and stale revisions in two tabs; retain unsaved text until server acknowledgement.

This does not promise background offline synchronization, browser-crash recovery, clinical decision support or a full production training catalogue.
