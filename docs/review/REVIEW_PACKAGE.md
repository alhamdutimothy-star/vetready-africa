## 2. Source classification and review procedure

Use multiple purpose tags, not one mutually exclusive category:

| Tag                             | Appropriate role                                         | Limits                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TRAINING CONTENT                | Explanation, demonstration, worked examples              | Usefulness does not authorize assessment thresholds.                                            |
| DOMAIN REFERENCE                | Professional background and methods                      | Confirm scope, edition, evidence and current applicability.                                     |
| FARM CASE                       | Contextual investigation and reporting exercise          | Permission and de-identification required; historical recommendation is not a universal answer. |
| ASSESSMENT SOURCE               | Candidate item/rubric input                              | Independently review accuracy, ambiguity, answer exposure, marking and fairness.                |
| PRACTICAL EXERCISE              | Observed task/checklist                                  | Validate safety, equipment, supervision and context.                                            |
| PRODUCT-SPECIFIC MATERIAL       | Authenticated product dossier, presentation or label     | Claims apply only to the verified product, version and authorized context.                      |
| FORMULATION/NUTRITION REFERENCE | Specifications, ingredient data and calculation examples | Preserve units, nutrient basis, analytical provenance and species/stage.                        |
| HISTORICAL FARM DATA            | Authentic past records                                   | Missing data, selection bias, privacy, dates and confounding must be recorded.                  |
| COMMERCIAL/SALES TRAINING       | Visits, territory, presentation and commercial process   | Does not establish clinical, nutritional or product-efficacy standards.                         |
| REQUIRES EXTERNAL VALIDATION    | Claims needing corroboration                             | Can coexist with any other tag.                                                                 |

When access is available:

1. Discover relevant files across dairy, poultry, nutrition/formulation, MG2MIX/additives, medicines/sales and professional/digital skills. Log query scope, accessible folders, pagination and inaccessible items; a sample is not an exhaustive inventory.
2. Record metadata before interpreting content. Modified-at is not document date; filename date is not automatically publication date. Unknown author/version remains unknown. Keep folder path plus stable source ID/link.
3. Read content and record exact page, slide or worksheet/range for each proposed use. File names alone do not establish relevance. Classify inspected, metadata-only, partially reviewed and unreadable separately.
4. For spreadsheets inspect units, formulas, hidden sheets, input provenance, errors and assumptions; do not execute macros or external links. For presentations/PDFs distinguish quotations, original work, historical examples and commercial claims.
5. Identify species/stage/system/geography, intended audience, conflicts/sponsorship, current applicability and rights. Connected access is not permission to republish another party's copyright or confidential farm/customer information.
6. Deduplicate identical files and relate revisions; keep each source version's identity/checksum when a copy is lawfully retained. Avoid blanket Drive download or ingestion into the application.
7. Map reviewed excerpts to child competencies and allowed uses. Seek expert and external verification where needed. No automatic bulk promotion to approved material.

## 3. Provenance and review-state design

Capture source filename/type/location, author/organization, date and date basis, version, species, production stage, geographical context, audience, pathway/competency relevance, product-specific flag, independent verification, review status, reviewer/date, inspected sections, rights/confidentiality and limitations. Add language, currency/units where relevant, source owner, sponsorship and supersession relationships.

The requested labels are useful, but represent different dimensions:

- Workflow: DRAFT → REQUIRES DOMAIN EXPERT REVIEW → reviewed; RETIRED when superseded or withdrawn.
- Provenance: INTERNAL PROFESSIONAL MATERIAL is origin/type, not proof of approval.
- Claim verification: EXTERNALLY VERIFIED requires identified claim, cited authoritative evidence, scope and reviewer. It should not mean every statement in a file is verified.
- Permitted use: APPROVED FOR TRAINING and APPROVED FOR ASSESSMENT are separate scoped decisions; a file can hold both or neither.

Record approval/rejection/revocation as attributed events with reasons, exact source version, permitted excerpt, use and applicable context. Approval for assessment content is separate from approval of thresholds, weights or practical gates. Those remain blocked.

## 4. Source hierarchy and product safeguards

| Claim/use                                    | Source priority to seek — not sources reviewed here                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Law, registration, medicine/feed permissions | Current applicable Nigerian authority and professional requirements                                                            |
| Animal-health/biosecurity methods            | Appropriate WOAH, FAO, professional references and peer-reviewed evidence                                                      |
| Nutrient systems and specifications          | Relevant NRC/NASEM or other recognized species/stage references, peer-reviewed evidence and qualified nutrition review         |
| Poultry management/KPIs                      | Appropriate current breeder guide plus local system context and reviewed definitions                                           |
| Dairy methods/KPIs                           | Recognized dairy technical/veterinary references with relevant breed/system applicability                                      |
| Product identity/composition/authorized use  | Authenticated current manufacturer documentation, applicable authorization and product/batch-specific analytical documentation |
| Product efficacy/generalized application     | Applicable independent evidence, study quality and contextual review; marketing slides alone are insufficient                  |

Authority is claim-specific, not a single universal ranking. A breeder target is not necessarily a Nigerian farm benchmark; an old guideline needs a currency check. A Certificate of Analysis supports specified results for its identified batch/method, not general efficacy or every future batch.

Foundational competence must be manufacturer-neutral. Example: interpreting mycotoxin risk remains a general professional task. Recommending a named product at its label inclusion is separate product training tied to manufacturer, label/version, species and market. Branded training never substitutes for independent reasoning, including a defensible no-product recommendation. Assessments must not reward brand recall over scientifically justified action.

## 6. Revised competency hierarchy

Professional domain → organizing group → stable assessable child → versioned learning outcomes/criteria.

Pathways and role profiles reference child versions; they are applicability maps, not parents that force duplicated identities. The 47 groups remain discoverable categories. Shared nutrition, presentation, negotiation, CRM and territory children reference more than one current group. Role-specific overlays select application context without cloning the underlying skill.

Do not award a group-level pass by averaging unrelated children. Keep a candidate's evidence-backed profile as criterion/task outcomes with context. Aggregate readiness requires an independently approved policy, which is not supplied here.

The child catalog contains 69 meaningful tasks. This is a proposed framework, not 69 lessons/exams to create immediately. A representative integrated assessment can cover multiple children. Specialist procedures, independent advanced formulation, equipment-specific skills and broad farm-size claims require additional role-specific validation rather than expansion by keyword.

## 9. Proposed baseline architecture

The baseline is diagnostic and modular, not a single employability examination.

| Component                        | Task modes                                                      | Output and limit                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Core knowledge                   | Reviewed MCQs/short responses                                   | Concepts known or missed; no pathway pass mark                                                          |
| Quantitative/digital             | Calculations, small datasets, workbook task                     | Accuracy, units, formulas and interpretation; separate spreadsheet execution from species KPI knowledge |
| Pathway reasoning                | Clinical/farm cases, reviewed images, information requests      | Reasoning, uncertainty and prioritized next actions; images alone do not prove practical skill          |
| Communication/reporting          | Written report, technical explanation, sales scenario           | Observable task output; human-reviewed rubric                                                           |
| Optional scheduled demonstration | Assessor-observed task appropriate to role and authorized scope | Provisional observation with context; not automatically field competence                                |
| Existing evidence intake         | Qualifications, work records, declared experience               | Authenticity/ownership review; separate from demonstrated competence                                    |

Candidates take core tasks plus selected pathway modules. Shared tasks need not be repeated solely because another pathway is selected; applicability must still be checked. Item/task versions link to child criteria and approved source excerpts. Record correctness or observed behavior only after content/answer-key review; do not invent passing criteria.

Report four panels: knowledge findings; interpretation/reasoning findings; observed demonstration; unverified or missing evidence. Label all diagnostic observations provisional unless a separately approved verification process applies. No global percentage, employability rank or JOB/FIELD READY classification.

Low-bandwidth design: text/data first, small files, optional compressed media, accessible alternatives, resumable task progress and acknowledged autosaves. Version/timestamp submissions, avoid offline duplication, provide interruption handling and appropriate accommodations. Safety-sensitive tasks need assessor supervision, identity checks and site permission. Calibrate reviewers and provide moderation/correction/appeal before high-stakes reuse.

## 10. Conceptual competency-gap engine

Candidate evidence → provenance/permission checks → authenticity/review → applicability to child version/context → approved requirement comparison → explained gap → development action.

Keep diagnostic evidence separate from verified competence. Suggested result reasons:

- NOT_ASSESSED / EVIDENCE_MISSING: no relevant evidence; not a statement of inability.
- UNDER_REVIEW: evidence awaiting an authorized decision.
- CONTEXT_MISMATCH: existing evidence does not establish the requested setting/species/task.
- NEEDS_DEVELOPMENT: an approved rubric identifies specific weaknesses; raw MCQ results alone cannot establish general inability.
- EXPIRED_OR_REVOKED: requires a reviewed validity rule or revocation event.
- POLICY_PENDING: the role requirement, transfer rule or verification policy is not approved.
- EVIDENCE_MEETS_APPROVED_RULE: future result only when the relevant requirement is approved and applicable evidence satisfies it.

Currently, professional requirement comparison must remain POLICY_PENDING wherever rules are unapproved. The engine can conceptually identify missing material or evidence, but cannot label a candidate professionally deficient against an invented threshold. Do not calculate final percentages.

An explanation identifies child/version, target role/context, evidence considered, mismatch/missing criterion, policy/source version, reviewer and recommended action. Employers cannot gain access to private evidence simply because it is useful for matching. Recompute after evidence correction/revocation and policy review without rewriting historical decisions.

## 11. Individual development plan

A plan has candidate, target pathway/role version, consent/access scope, baseline snapshot, evidenced strengths, provisional findings, gap reasons, activities, prerequisites, mentor/assessor support, delivery constraints and review history.

Each item records: target child/criterion/context, reason, approved learning resource version, activity type, task output expected, reassessment route, responsible support person, scheduling preference and state. Completion of a lesson closes learning activity only; assessment/verification is separate. No prerequisite, priority or deadline is invented as a professional standard.

Illustrative scenario only, not an actual candidate: a veterinarian targets poultry management and has corroborated clinical/biosecurity evidence but insufficient layer analysis, spreadsheet, budgeting and management evidence. Plan: reviewed layer dataset exercise → spreadsheet task → poultry budget case → staff/SOP simulation → relevant supervised farm assessment. Existing evidence is reused only when its scope matches. The farmer/farm-scale context must be explicit. An unavailable placement remains a logistical constraint, not automatically a failing competence judgment.

Prioritize with candidate/mentor input, safety and approved prerequisites, rather than opaque AI or unvalidated weighted scores. On review, update activities as new evidence arrives; retain reasons/history. Opportunity-specific plans later map vacancy requirements to the same children and avoid duplicating training merely because several vacancies request the same skill.
