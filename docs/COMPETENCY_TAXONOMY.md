# Proposed four-pathway competency taxonomy

**REQUIRES DOMAIN EXPERT VALIDATION**

This is a draft taxonomy for founder and expert review, not an approved credentialing standard. Seeded entries group outcomes so the platform engine can be exercised without pretending to contain a complete curriculum. Passing thresholds, weights, mandatory gates, required levels and expiry remain NULL. Author/reviewer/source fields await named expert contributions.

## Professional outcome coverage for review

### Commercial Dairy Practice

Groups must ultimately cover herd health, calf health, heifer development, transition management, fresh-cow monitoring, reproduction, mastitis, lameness/locomotion, BCS, metabolic disease, vaccination, biosecurity, milk/fertility KPIs, basic nutrition, forage/TMR, records, economics, investigation, reporting and farmer/staff communication. Assess herd-level reasoning in addition to individual animal examinations.

Proposed observed evidence: BCS, locomotion, calf examination, mastitis investigation, farm audit, transition-cow assessment, KPI interpretation and farm report. The reviewer determines which tasks and context are mandatory; no seed asserts a professional minimum.

### Veterinary Pharmaceutical Technical Sales

Cover pharmacology, dossier-backed product knowledge/use, antimicrobial stewardship, pharmacovigilance, needs assessment, positioning, presentation, territory/segmentation, CRM, pipeline, objections, negotiation, ethics, customer visits, competitor analysis, forecasts, reporting and follow-up. Assess applied ethical commercial performance separately from pharmacological knowledge; volume sold is not an ethical competence proxy.

Proposed observed evidence: product presentation, customer-needs assessment, objections, ethical recommendation and visit simulation. Species, approved dossier, context and authorized use must be recorded.

### Poultry Farm Management

Production: broiler/layer/pullet systems, brooding, density, feeding/water, lighting, ventilation, temperature, litter, weighing, uniformity and planning.
Health: observation, mortality investigation, supervised necropsy, disease recognition, vaccination/medication plans, biosecurity, prevention, stewardship and health records.
Nutrition: intake/FCR, energy, crude protein, digestible amino acids, calcium, available phosphorus, sodium, fibre/fat, quality, phases/additives, mycotoxins and heat stress. This is production literacy, not qualification as a feed formulator.
Layer analytics: hen-day and hen-housed production, egg weight/mass, intake, FCR per egg mass, mortality/livability, body weight/uniformity, egg quality/cracks/dirt, feed cost per egg and persistency.
Broiler analytics: ADG, weight, FCR, intake, mortality/livability, uniformity, EPEF/efficiency, feed cost per kg gain, cost per kg live weight, water:feed and condemnation where relevant.
Operations: people/SOPs, inventory/feed/medicines, vaccination scheduling, records/reporting, mortality records, biosecurity compliance, procurement, budgeting/cost control and production forecasting. Digital tasks use spreadsheets, charts, KPI dashboards and investigation.

Proposed observed evidence: house inspection, records analysis, mortality investigation, supervised necropsy where lawful, biosecurity audit, feed/water assessment, flock-performance analysis, staff/SOP scenario and farm report.

KPI definitions must state numerator, denominator, units, period, live/housed population, mortality accounting and data limitations. Reviewers validate formulas and benchmark sources before software calculation. Do not seed default nutrient targets or production benchmarks.

### Premix & Feed Additives Technical Sales

Nutrition: energy/protein/amino acids/digestibility, minerals/vitamins, Ca/P/Na, fibre/fat, intake/conversion and nutrient density.
Ingredients: maize, soybean meal/cake, groundnut cake, wheat offal, maize bran, rice bran, PKC, limestone, DCP, bone meal, vegetable oils and salt; composition/quality variability and lawful use must be assessed.
Premixes: vitamin-mineral systems, inclusion rates, carriers/micro-ingredients, storage/handling, weighing/mixing, trace minerals, stability and consequences of incorrect inclusion.
Additives: enzymes/phytase, mycotoxin-control products, acids, probiotics/prebiotics, hepatoprotective products, antioxidants, emulsifiers, pigments, gut-health and electrolytes. Mechanistic plausibility is not proof of efficacy; distinguish reviewed evidence, species/context and unsupported marketing claims.
Formulation literacy: kg/ton, nutrient specification, substitutions, cost/ton, premix/additive inclusion, energy/protein/amino-acid relationships, Ca/available P and feed-cost implications; no advanced formulation qualification implied.
Performance: poultry intake/FCR/egg output/egg mass/mortality/body weight/uniformity/feed cost; dairy yield/components when available/DMI/BCS/fertility/transition/calf/metabolic indicators.
Mill: receiving/storage, weighing, grinding, mixing/sequencing, micro-addition, carryover, sampling, QA and traceability.
Commercial: segmentation/prospecting, needs assessment, farm/mill audit, positioning, presentation/demonstration, objections/comparison, pricing/value, ROI, trial design/monitoring, follow-up, CRM, territory and forecasting.

Proposed observed evidence: farm/mill audit, formulation/label interpretation, product presentation, needs assessment, objections/comparison, trial design/results, ROI demonstration and technical report.

Case principle: investigate declining layer production, shell quality and feed-cost changes using flock age/history, intake, formulation/ingredients, Ca/P, inclusion/mixing, disease, water, heat and management before recommending a product. A defensible outcome can be “no product indicated.” Trials require controls, defined outcomes, confounder handling, permissions and safe lawful use; approved reviewers determine practical trial expectations.

## Role-specific layer

Role profiles overlay core + pathway requirements without cloning competencies. Examples for later reviewed role definitions: dairy herd veterinarian; poultry farm manager for a specified flock scale/system; pharmaceutical field representative; premix/additives representative serving farms versus feed mills. Farm scale is contextual evidence/experience, not a keyword granting suitability.

## Evidence and readiness labels

Distinguish self-declared, document verified, knowledge assessed, case assessed and practically verified. These are evidence facets, not a universal ordered ladder; a document check cannot certify skill. Evidence may also reference verified employment, internship/externship, mentor assessment and moderated employer feedback. Those inputs alone cannot replace mandatory observed practical tasks.

Configured readiness catalog: NOT ASSESSED, FOUNDATION, DEVELOPING, KNOWLEDGE VERIFIED, CASE VERIFIED, PRACTICALLY VERIFIED, JOB READY, FIELD READY, ADVANCED. A policy evaluates each pathway/role independently. FIELD READY always requires approved practical gates; high examination scores cannot substitute. Mentor is a role/capability, not a pilot readiness label.
