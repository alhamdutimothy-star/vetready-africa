# Dairy Pilot Verification Configuration Design v1.0

This increment defines inert, versioned drafts for the approved Dairy Pilot 1 working architecture and the common verification rubric. It has no publication path or runtime consumer. Structural completeness is a review aid. An external reference recorded in a draft does not prove approval, authority or evidence.

## Configuration and scope

A package identifies its version, predecessor, provenance and architecture and framework versions. It defines R01–R11 exactly once as required default Field-Ready gates. Each gate pins an exact default scope and may define separate narrower scopes linked to that default. Each scope pins its rubric and policy version and records exact pathway and child versions once approved. The validator checks references within the package. Resolution of external records, semantic review of scope text, approval and immutable version storage belong to a separate governed publication design.

R07 is bounded to first-line reproductive clinical assessment. R11 retains distinct components for feed access, water access, feeding conditions, BCS, rumen fill, manure, rumination, contextual interpretation and specialist referral. The scope labels do not define methods, clinical procedures or permissions. A narrower scope remains distinct even when it uses the same component names with more limited clinical boundaries. It cannot replace the default scope.

Rubrics pin D01–D11 criteria and distinguish mandatory, supporting and inapplicable criteria. Each required component must have a mandatory direct practical criterion when criteria are supplied. A designated safety-critical criterion must be mandatory and supported by direct practical evidence. Approved evidence policies will specify routes, coverage, provenance, assistance and currency. This draft format can describe in-person and direct remote observation routes, subject to approval. It does not admit document review or simulation as a substitute for direct practical observation.

## Future workflow boundaries

Level A is Knowledge Assessed, Level B is Case Assessed and Level C is Practically Verified. Learning, self-declaration, case work and assessor confidence cannot satisfy a direct practical requirement. A visit may provide shared provenance for several competencies. Each competency needs its own documented findings, exact rubric and separate authorised decision. Evidence quality, assessor confidence and the decision remain separate. Approved safety-critical failures block positive decisions for the affected scope. Restrictions, moderation, reassessment and restoration must preserve prior decisions and their supporting authority.

Any future runtime consumer must preserve the existing governed decision chain and immutable evidence receipts. The default Field-Ready projection would require all eleven full gates to have effective Level C evidence and meet approved safety requirements. Supervised entry may show a mixture of A, B and individually verified C capabilities with explicit supervision and limits. A narrow claim cannot satisfy a full gate.

The proposed employer claim shape permits competency, level, scope, decision date, effectiveness, limitations and reassessment due date. The shape rejects extra fields. It neither sanitises arbitrary display text nor grants access. A future projection must supply approved display text and apply the bounded consent policy before disclosure.
