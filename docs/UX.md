# Mobile-first screen plan

Shared: accessible role-aware navigation, saved-state indicator, text-first screens, clear verification labels, 360px Android layout, no autoplay media and retry/resume controls.

## Candidate

Onboarding: staged qualification/registration, location, relocation, availability, pathway, experience, languages, driving/digital skills, optional CV; save/resume and private defaults.
Dashboard: evidence coverage, role-specific readiness, unmet gates and next development activity.
Training: small lessons, optional compressed captioned media, progress checkpoints.
Assessment: rubric instructions, server timing, autosave confirmation, structured case report and submission receipt.
Competency profile: distinguish diagnostic, self-reported and verified results; show method, level, reviewer, expiry and appeal.
Skills Passport: preview the recipient's exact fields; choose audience, sections and expiry; separate contact permission; revoke shares.
Jobs/applications: verified employer, salary currency/period, explained fit and missing requirements, application consent and visible status.

## Employer

Dashboard with verification and active recruiting. Vacancy form distinguishes essential gates from optional skills, specifies pathway/location/species/pay and willingness to train. Talent search filters consented profiles by verified competence, readiness, availability and location compatibility. Comparison displays evidence coverage and explanations rather than public ranking. Pipeline records application, shortlist, invitation, interview, offer, placement, rejection and withdrawal; notes are organization-scoped.

## Assessor, trainer and staff

Assessor queue shows assignments, scope/conflicts, identity/context, rubric anchors, evidence, critical errors, feedback and moderation. Trainer drafts content and gives feedback; publication requires review. Administration manages users/roles, content versions, competencies, employers, vacancies, assessment allocation, appeals, placements, privacy requests, audit and aggregate analytics. Suppress small identifying geographic cohorts.

Acceptance: on mobile a candidate can resume a lesson, submit a case and explain remaining readiness gaps; an employer can compare permissioned evidence and request contact without premature disclosure.
